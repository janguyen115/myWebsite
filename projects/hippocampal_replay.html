Background

As a member of the 2024 cohort for the Socal Data Science Research Fellowship (REU) at UC Irvine, I had the opportunity to work alongside Dr. Babak Shahbaba, Dr. Norbert Fortin, and Zhishun (Zoey) Yang on a project examining the phenomenon of hippocampal replay of nonspatial sequences of events in rats. Alongside my collaborators, I analyzed electrophysiological data recorded from the hippocampus of rats as they performed a odor-based sequence memory task. Much of this project involved developing and implementing statistical methods flexible enough to capture nonlinearities in the data whilst simultaneously being robust enough to account for the spatiotemporal heterogeneity and high dimensionality of the data. As a research assistant in the Fortin Lab, I was tasked with generating a priori probabilisitc simulations to which we could compare our own results to analyze pointwise correlations. As I continue to study methods and problems I encountered in this project, I anticipate further opportunities to explore biologically inspired statistical methods.
