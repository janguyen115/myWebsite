A Student's Guide to Numerical Bayes (with examples)

"A Student's Guide to Numerical Bayes" is a resource that I am currently developing based on "Computational Bayesian Statistics -- An Introduction" by M.A. Amaral Turkman, C.D. Paulino, and P. Mueller. It is my attempt to simplify to others (and to myself) the concepts and methods of numerical solutions to Bayesian inference in a more accessible and intuitive way. My goal is to provide timely examples that develop concepts gradually and to reveal step-by-step solutions to examples from the textbook. This guide is a work in progress and likely contains mistakes; please contact me to report any errors or to provide feedback.
